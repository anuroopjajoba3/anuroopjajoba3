## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer and Co-Founder** building production AI systems that solve real problems.

Most recently I built **ClinicalMind** — a 7-agent LangGraph platform that autonomously searches **35M+ PubMed papers**, runs pharmacovigilance checks against a patient's medication list, and produces GRADE-assessed clinical reports in **under 60 seconds**.

I also co-founded **PaySplit**, a fintech platform with LangChain agentic workflows, Stripe + Plaid integrations, and production infrastructure on **AWS ECS Fargate at 99.9%+ uptime**.

> Most engineers use AI tools. I build them.
